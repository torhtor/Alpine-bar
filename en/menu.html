Alpine × Christian Drouin

Founded in 1960, Maison Christian Drouin is located in the heart of Pays d'Auge. It perpetuates a family savoir-faire combining tradition and innovation, with rigorous apple selection and artisanal distillation in small stills. From calvados to gins, each creation expresses the richness of Norman terroir, offering elegant, complex and authentic spirits.
